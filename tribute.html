Tribute to compassplate

For those who do not know, I (Josh) was not the original creator of compassplate. The original owner, Hunter, passed away this year in Febuary. He was a personal friend of mine, and he ended his own life at the age of 17 due to school struggles and constantly struggling. I wasn't aware of this, and he never talked to anyone about anything he was going through. His mother shared with me a video she found in his apartment when he passed, and it contained apologies to many of his friends, including instructions for me.

Hunter was a good friend, he never told anyone about his struggles. I will always remember him and the impact he had on my life. I wish I could have done more to help him. The site will remain active (unless the Federal Government takes down the site again) in respect to his hard work.

Rest in peace, Hunter. You will be missed.

Tribute to Hunter - Written by Josh
